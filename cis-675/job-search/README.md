# Job Search — Team 4

US6: discover relevant opportunities through skills, including roles candidates may not think to search for. Grounded in the Part 1 introduction's recognition of traditional qualifications and overlooked transferable experience.

All companies, jobs, salaries, and connections are fictional fixtures, not real vacancies or recommendations. The product UI is presented as a finished experience. Keyword/location filters, skill connections, detail dialogs, and saving are browser-local illustrations. No external job feed, real matching, or application submission is implemented.

The page reads candidate-confirmed skills from the My Profile prototype when available. Each job exposes required and preferred qualifications, and a skills connection does not imply all requirements are met.

Shared persona maps include My Profile and Job Search for Job seeker and Job Posting for Hiring manager. New pages must follow ../AGENTS.md.

JavaScript syntax checked. Browser runtime unavailable for visual testing.
