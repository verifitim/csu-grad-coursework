# Candidate Matches — Team 4

Fictional hiring manager prototype supporting US4 (recognize capable candidates with unexpected backgrounds) and US9 (explain candidate fit), grounded in Part 1's conventional and transferable qualification value proposition.

Includes a job selector, job-specific required/preferred criteria, conventional and transferable candidate backgrounds, supporting evidence and outstanding questions, a candidate review dialog, and per-job shortlisting.

The primary job uses the locally saved Job Posting when available. A second fictional Training Coordinator posting illustrates changing jobs. Candidate records, evidence, and explanations are fixed fixtures. Simple text rules associate sample evidence with criteria; this is not actual candidate evaluation, verification, scoring, or an AI matching engine. Custom qualifications may require manual review. No hiring action or external notification occurs. All shortlists are browser-local.

Alex Morgan is the same fictional retail candidate across pages. Available browser-local profile details and confirmed skills are reflected on Candidate Matches. Supporting qualification evidence is still scripted; edits do not trigger general-purpose analysis. No real people or jobs are represented.

Required evidence counts show available sample evidence, not a fit percentage or verified fulfillment. Human review remains explicit. Evaluation approach changes which evidence is emphasized (qualifications and history, demonstrated abilities, or both). Candidate inclusion is not automatically changed, and no real screening or ranking is performed.

All four pages register Candidate Matches in Hiring manager navigation. Future pages must follow ../AGENTS.md.

Validation: JavaScript syntax, unique IDs, registered paths, and navigation map consistency. Browser execution environment remains unavailable.
